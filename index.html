FAQ
Common questions
Do I need a Claude API key?
Yes. You need an Anthropic API key (free to get at console.anthropic.com). The cost is tiny — roughly $0.001 per message with Claude Haiku. We don't mark it up. Your key stays on your device.
Is my LinkedIn data safe?
Yes. LinkedInAI reads the profile you have open and sends only that data to the Claude API to generate your message. We never store your LinkedIn activity, your messages, or your contacts.
Does it work in languages other than English?
Yes. Claude supports most major languages. Just describe your goal in the custom instructions field in your language, and it will generate messages accordingly.
Will LinkedIn detect this?
LinkedInAI generates text only — it does not auto-send anything. You always review, copy, and paste the message yourself. There's nothing for LinkedIn to detect.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes, cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period, then drop to the free tier.
