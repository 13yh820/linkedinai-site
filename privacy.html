Privacy Policy

LinkedInAI – AI Message Generator
Last updated: June 27, 2026

1. Overview

LinkedInAI is a Chrome extension that helps you generate personalized LinkedIn messages using the Anthropic Claude AI API. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Collect

LinkedInAI collects the following data:

  • LinkedIn profile information – Name, headline, and role visible on the LinkedIn profile page you are currently viewing. This data is read from your browser tab and sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate messages. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Anthropic API Key – Stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage count – A monthly message count stored locally in your browser. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • License key – If you purchase a Pro plan, your license key is stored locally in your browser. Never transmitted to our servers.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your LinkedIn credentials.
  • We do not track your browsing history.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, except Anthropic (for AI message generation).
  • We do not use cookies or analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

LinkedInAI uses the Anthropic Claude API to generate messages. Profile data from the current LinkedIn page is sent to Anthropic's API for this purpose. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

5. Data Storage

All user data (API key, usage count, license key) is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage. No data is transmitted to or stored on LinkedInAI's servers.

6. Permissions

LinkedInAI requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab – To read the LinkedIn profile page you are currently viewing.
  • scripting – To extract profile information from the LinkedIn page.
  • storage – To store your API key and usage data locally.

7. Children's Privacy

LinkedInAI is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact
